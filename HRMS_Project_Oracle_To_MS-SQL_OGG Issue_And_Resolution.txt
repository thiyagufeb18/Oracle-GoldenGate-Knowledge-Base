
Today's GG Issue and Resolution
********************************

We configured Bi-directional replication between Oracle and MS-SQL.
Oracle and Ms-SQL Tables structures are NON-IDENTICAL and we made MS-SQL PK column as "Identity column".
We tried to test DML operations from MS-SQL to Oracle; 5 Insert statements were executed.
2 insert statements were captured after that EXTRACT did not capture Single SQL, 
After stopping Primary Extract, we attempted to restart it. The CDC Capture Job was not operating, and it was continuously abending.  

We do not have any clue in Extract report file, ggserr log file including DB Log file.
40+ hrs Oracle DB was out of sync with MS-SQL !!

When started our analysis from  GG*_CT table.
We observed that insert statements were not captured by EXTRACT within the *_CT Tables.

Resolution : 
**************

Our approach has started ...

1) Capture Job & Cleanup Job deleted and Re-created.
2) Cleanup done existing GoldenGate metadata tables.
3) Recreated the channel and CDC jobs.

1) To delete and recreate capture Jobs 
-----------------------------------------

exec sp_cdc_drop_job 'cleanup';
exec sp_cdc_drop_job 'capture';
EXEC sys.sp_cdc_create_db                        -->  To enable Change Data Capture at the database level.
EXEC sys.sp_cdc_add_job @job_type = N'capture';  -->  Creates the job that will process the transaction log for changes.
EXEC sys.sp_cdc_start_job @job_type = 'capture'; -->  To begin capturing changes.


2) cleanedup existing GoldenGate metadata tables.
---------------------------------------------------
ggadmin.ggcheck_lox
ggadmin.OracleGGExtractCheckpoint
ggadmin.OracleGGTranTables
ggadmin.OracleGGCleanupJobStatus

3) Trandata deleted for business tables and ggadmin.checkpoint table

Checkpoint table deleted --> ggadmin.checkpoint
Trandata deleted for 700+ tables (all tables part of the database) including *_CT Tables.

4) GG Processes were deleted and recreated. 

=> Extract process was deleted and recreated. 
=> Replicat processes were deleted and recreated. 

5) Unable to bringup Replicat & Extract processes for Various reasons.

Reason for the issue :
---------------------- 
Identity column data was not handled properly on MS-SQL Side.
Just disabled P.Key and manually inserted duplicate values for auto increment column which is PK column.
(Duplicate values inserted for auto increment column) so CDC Capture job was unable to capture Sub-sequent transactions.
